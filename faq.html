QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

PromptSnip 2.0 FAQ.

Short answers to the questions users are most likely to have before or after installing the extension.

What can I use as input in PromptSnip 2.0?

You can use webpage selections, images, text, PDFs, supported video and audio input, plus supported public URL workflows.

Can I use more than one image?

Yes. The Image workflow supports both single-image and multiple-image input.

Does PromptSnip choose the prompt type automatically?

It can automatically select a relevant prompt type from your input, while still letting you change that choice manually.

Is PromptSnip free?

The current free plan provides up to 15 generations per day and 450 monthly credits.

What happens when I run out of credits?

The Credits section shows your current usage and provides a way to request additional credits.

Do I need an account?

Yes. PromptSnip uses Google sign-in to connect your account securely.

Can I regenerate a prompt?

Yes. Regenerate lets you request another result without rebuilding the entire source workflow.

Can I revisit old prompts?

Yes. The History tab is designed so you can return to previously generated prompts and reuse them.

Which browser does it support?

PromptSnip is currently distributed through the Google Chrome Web Store.
